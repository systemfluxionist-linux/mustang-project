## Observable Visual Notes (from model metadata & GT3 spec)

### Proportions
- **GT3-class silhouette:** Long hood, short rear deck, cabin set back — typical front-mid-engine proportions
- **Wheelbase:** Extended relative to road car; front wheels pushed forward for aero balance
- **Overhangs:** Minimal front overhang (splitter-led), moderate rear overhang with diffuser
- **Track width:** Visibly wider than production Mustang; flared arches cover GT3-spec slicks
- **Ride height:** Low, rake-forward stance (front splitter near ground, rear slightly higher)

### Stance
- **Aggressive rake:** Pronounced nose-down attitude; front splitter leading edge lowest point
- **Wide track:** Front and rear fenders bulge outward; tires fill arches nearly flush
- **Squat:** Rear diffuser and wing suggest high-downforce setup; rear sits on springs stiff for aero stability
- **Wheel fitment:** Deep-dish center-lock wheels; Michelin/Pirelli GT3 slicks visible sidewall lettering

### Color / Livery
- **Base:** Ford Performance "Velocity Blue" (deep metallic blue) — primary factory GT3 color
- **Accents:** White/red pinstriping along beltline and splitter edges
- **Number panels:** White door rounds with black race numbers (Proton Competition / Ford Performance style)
- **Badging:** Ford oval on nose, Mustang script on rear, GT3 class decals on doors
- **Sponsors:** Subtle Michelin, Ford Performance, Proton Competition logos (per 2024 WEC/IMSA spec)

### Grille & Front Fascia
- **Mesh grille:** Full-width honeycomb mesh behind lower opening; blacked-out surround
- **Splitter:** Carbon-fiber front splitter extends full width; center keel + outer strakes
- **Canards:** Dual-element dive planes on front bumper corners (adjustable mount visible)
- **Headlights:** Production-style LED units retained but with yellow endurance-racing film
- **Brake ducts:** Large NACA-style inlets in lower bumper feeding front brakes
- **Hood vents:** Twin extractors aft of front wheels; louvered carbon fiber

### Aero Details (observable)

- **Diffuser:** Full-width carbon diffuser; center section deeper, outer strakes curved
- **Side skirts:** Sealed to floor; vortex generators ahead of rear wheels

- **Mirrors:** Carbon stalks, aero-shaped housings, wide-angle glass

### Interior (glimpsed via windows)
